⚽About MLD 03Where the name comes from+

MLD 03 traces its roots to a simple eFootball community inspired by the football legend Paolo Maldini.

It was founded by WaCky, a passionate Maldini fan, as the Maldini Friendly Group. Over time the name was shortened to MLD, and later became MLD 03: the “03” is a tribute to Maldini's iconic number 3 shirt.

What started as a friendly gaming group grew into a competitive community, and today it is MLD 03 Esports.

From friendly matches to organised tournaments, MLD keeps bringing players together through competition, consistency and a shared love of eFootball.
